    An engine that composes, not just reacts
    
      
    
        Most audio visualisers map loudness to a shape and leave it there. ASCII Broadcast
        analyses each track first — tempo, meter, sections, energy, brightness — and writes a
        visual score: chapters, scenes, transitions, titles, signal effects and memory
        marks laid out along the whole programme before a single frame is drawn.
      
    
      
    
        Scenes are authored worlds, not filters. Folded City builds architecture out of
        punctuation. Weaving Engine grows a lattice that remembers what it made. Negative Space
        Theatre withholds, then pays off. Motifs return. Chapter A′ recalls chapter A. It reads
        as a piece, because it is composed as one.
      
    
      
      
        
    • Deterministic and seeded — the same playlist renders the same show.
      • 
        
    • Reacts inside a plan: the beat drives the surface, the score drives the arc.
      • 
        
    • Degrades gracefully: detail is shed before timing is.
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    FOLDED CITY
    Concourses, signage and platforms assembled from │ ─ ┌ ╱ ▓. Perspective is drawn, never photographed.
    
      
    WEAVING ENGINE
    A lattice that accumulates, stresses and releases. Rhythmic density becomes literal structure.
    
      
    NEGATIVE SPACE
    Emptiness as an instrument. The field holds its breath so the drop has somewhere to land.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    A real broadcast pipeline, on your machine
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Native encode
    
        
    VideoToolbox H.264 High at 1080p30 and AAC-LC 44.1 kHz, muxed to FLV and published over RTMPS. The program bus runs at 48 kHz; the single sample-rate conversion happens on the way out.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Two honest states
    
        
    The studio reports what it is doing — SENDING — and what the destination says — LIVE STATUS UNVERIFIED — as separate values. It will never tell you that you are live when it only knows a socket is open.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Recording is a safety net
    
        
    A local MP4 records alongside the stream. If the network drops, the encoder keeps running, the reconnect backs off, the music never stops, and you still have the session.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Preflight before air
    
        
    Sources, analysis coverage, score, destination shape, stream key, disk headroom and frame budget are all checked. Every blocking item says what is wrong and the single action that clears it.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Photosensitivity safeguards
    
        
    Luminance flashes are limited to three per second, full-frame inversions are suppressed, and Reduce Motion clamps the camera grammar. On by default.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Your keys stay yours
    
        
    Stream keys live in the keychain — never in the session file, logs, diagnostics or exported presets. Presets export the look, not your library.
